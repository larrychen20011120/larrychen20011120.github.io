Hello 👋! I'm Kuan-Ting Chen, but you can call me Larry. I'm currently a master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), and I previously earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Information Engineering from National Cheng Kung University (NCKU).

My research interests span agentic AI, data mining, large language models, and reinforcement learning. I am a dedicated student with a strong academic record and an enthusiasm for emerging technologies. I am currently exploring the Hermes Agent framework and AI applications in finance.

Looking ahead, I hope to publish at top-tier conferences, contribute to open-source projects, and build practical AI-powered tools. I also enjoy data science and AI competitions, which help me sharpen my intuition for data and strengthen my problem-solving skills.
