About

Currently building sports intelligence

Current research focuses include sports intelligence, LLM agents, and tactical simulation.

Hi! I am Kuan-Ting Chen, a master's student in Computer Science at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, advised by Prof. Wen-Chih Peng.

My research sits at the intersection of sports analytics, large language models, and reinforcement learning. I build intelligent systems that can understand tactical context, simulate human decisions, and support better coaching and analysis.

Previously, I studied Computer Science at National Cheng Kung University, where I worked on natural language processing, hateful meme detection, and fact checking. I enjoy turning research ideas into systems that work beyond the lab.
