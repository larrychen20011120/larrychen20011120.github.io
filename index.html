Hello 👋! I'm Kuan-Ting Chen, but you can call me Larry. Looking ahead, I hope to publish at top-tier conferences, contribute to open-source projects, and build practical AI-powered tools. I also enjoy data science and AI competitions, which help me sharpen my intuition for data and strengthen my problem-solving skills.
